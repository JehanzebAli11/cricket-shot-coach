## What this is about

We would like to film you batting in the nets or on the ground. A computer
program will look at the videos to find the positions of your head, arms,
legs and bat. We use this to build a tool that gives batters feedback on
their shots.

Taking part is **your choice**. Saying no will not affect your place in the
academy, your selection, or how your coach treats you.

## What we will film

* Two phone cameras on stands, one from the side and one from the front.
* Only you batting. We try not to film anyone else.
* We do not record sound.

## How we use and store the videos

* Videos are kept on an encrypted drive that only the project team can open.
* Instead of your name we use a code (like "B017"). The list that links
  codes to names is kept separately and locked.
* From the videos we make "keypoints", which are a set of dots for your body
  position. These don't show your face.
* We keep the videos until the project ends, or **for up to 3 years**,
  whichever comes first, unless you agree in Part B to more.
* We will never sell your videos or give them to advertisers.

## Face blurring

We can blur your face in any video that anyone outside the project team sees.
Tick below if you want this.

## Your right to withdraw

You can change your mind **at any time, without giving a reason**. Tell your
coach or contact us using the details above. We will delete your videos and
keypoints within 30 days and confirm this to you. If your data has already
been used to train a computer model, we cannot remove it from that copy of
the model, but we will leave it out of every future version.
